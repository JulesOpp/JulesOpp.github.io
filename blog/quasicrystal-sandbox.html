The model

The Lifshitz–Petrich model describes a soft-matter quasicrystal with a scalar density field ρ(r) and the free energy

F = ∫ { c⁄2 [(∇² + 1)(∇² + q²) ρ]² − ε⁄2 ρ² − 1⁄3 ρ³ + 1⁄4 ρ⁴ } dr

where ε acts as a reduced temperature and c sets the strength of the gradient term, which favours wavenumbers 1 and q. The field follows overdamped Langevin dynamics,

∂ρ/∂t = −δF/δρ + √(2T(r)) η,

where η is white noise and the brush sets the local temperature T(r).
